💼 About the Marketing Department

The Department of Marketing at Government Brojomohan College is dedicated to developing future business leaders by offering a mix of traditional marketing knowledge and modern digital tools. Our curriculum, guided by experienced faculty, focuses on strategic thinking, communication, analytics, and real-world application.

  • 📘 Industry-oriented curriculum aligned with modern marketing trends
  • 🎯 Focus on digital marketing, branding, and consumer behavior
  • 🧑‍🏫 Experienced faculty including BCS-qualified lecturers
  • 🌐 Workshops, seminars, and industry talks to enhance real-world exposure
