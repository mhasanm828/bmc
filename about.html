About Govt. Brojomohun College, Barisal

A legacy of excellence, culture, and education since 1889.

🏛️ College Overview

Govt. Brojomohun College, Barisal, established in 1889, is one of the oldest and most prestigious educational institutions in southern Bangladesh. Affiliated with the National University of Bangladesh, BM College offers Honours and Masters programs in various disciplines across Arts, Science, Business Studies, and Social Sciences.

🎓 Academic Excellence

With over 10,000+ students and 30+ departments, the college provides quality education guided by experienced faculty members. The institution emphasizes both academic learning and extracurricular development.

📍 Campus & Infrastructure

Located in the heart of Barisal city, the campus is spread over a lush, green area. It includes modern classrooms, science laboratories, libraries, student hostels, an auditorium, and a mosque. The campus remains active with cultural programs, student initiatives, and national celebrations.

👨‍🏫 Vision & Mission

The college's mission is to promote knowledge, ethics, and leadership. It strives to produce responsible citizens ready to face global challenges while preserving cultural heritage and values.
