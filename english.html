About the Department

The English Department at Government BM College has been a cornerstone of literary education since its establishment in 1965. We pride ourselves on offering comprehensive programs that explore the richness of English literature while developing critical thinking and communication skills.

Our faculty consists of renowned scholars and dedicated educators who bring diverse perspectives to the study of literature, linguistics, and composition. The department serves as a vibrant intellectual community where students engage with texts from Shakespeare to postcolonial literature.

Key Features

  • Bachelor's and Master's degree programs
  • Annual literary festival and symposium
  • Research opportunities for students
  • Modern language lab facilities

Our Mission & Vision

Mission

To foster a deep appreciation for English literature and language, while developing critical thinkers, effective communicators, and engaged global citizens through rigorous academic programs, innovative teaching methods, and meaningful scholarship.

Vision

To be recognized as a premier center for English studies in Bangladesh, known for academic excellence, transformative learning experiences, and significant contributions to literary scholarship and cultural understanding.
